Air pollution and heat interact: hotter days often trap more pollution near the ground. Cleaner air is also climate action: less fossil fuel burning means fewer greenhouse gases and fewer toxic particles in our lungs.

Global warming doesn’t only mean “a bit warmer”. It shifts the odds: more heatwaves, heavier downpours, higher seas, stronger storms. Local data helps us ask: what are the main risks where I live?
